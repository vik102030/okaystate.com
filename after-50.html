This is not only about you.

The world has changed. We haven’t.

Old rules no longer work the same way.

This is not an individual failure. It’s a mass condition.

You’re not weaker. You’re more sensitive.

The illusion that everyone else is fine is just that — an illusion.

This is not about quick decisions.

You are not in a crisis. You are in a transition.

Something has stopped matching.

You are not required to know what to do next.

You stopped. You looked inside. You didn’t run.

For many people, this never happens.

You are not here by accident.
